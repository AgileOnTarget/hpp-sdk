# Governance — Human Presence Protocol (HPP)

The Human Presence Protocol (HPP) is a foundational protocol for establishing verifiable biological presence as a network primitive.

HPP is governed as an infrastructure standard, not a product.

This document defines how decisions are made, how maintainers are selected, and how the protocol evolves.

---

## Guiding Principles

- Stability over novelty
- Correctness over speed
- Minimalism over feature growth
- Privacy by default
- Deterministic behavior

HPP evolves slowly and intentionally. Breaking changes are exceptional.

---

## Maintainer Council

HPP is stewarded by a Maintainer Council. The council is responsible for:

- Reviewing specification changes
- Approving draft revisions
- Overseeing security posture
- Maintaining architectural coherence

The council acts as stewards, not owners.

---

## Maintainer Selection

Maintainers are selected based on:

- Demonstrated technical contributions
- History of rigorous review
- Understanding of protocol design
- Respect for HPP's guiding principles

Maintainers are invited by existing maintainers. There is no automatic promotion.

---

## Decision Making

Consensus-driven. When consensus cannot be reached:

- Discussion continues
- Alternatives are documented
- Changes may be deferred

Voting is a last resort. Simple majority decides.

---

## Change Categories

### Editorial

- Clarifications
- Typos
- Formatting

May be merged by a single maintainer.

### Minor Technical

- Tightening definitions
- Additional examples
- Non-breaking clarifications

Require two maintainer approvals.

### Major Technical

- Behavioral changes
- New primitives
- Security model changes

Require:

- Design proposal
- Public discussion
- Maintainer council consensus

---

## Backward Compatibility

Backward compatibility is a core value.

If a change breaks compatibility:

- Justification must be explicit
- Migration path must be provided
- Change must be approved by council consensus

---

## Forking Policy

HPP is open source. Forks are permitted. However:

Only this repository represents the canonical specification. Compatibility claims by forks are their responsibility.

---

## Conflict Resolution

Technical disagreements are resolved by:

- Reference to documented principles
- Reference to security and privacy impact
- Reference to simplicity and determinism

Personal attacks are not tolerated.

---

## Transparency

All decisions occur in public issues or pull requests. Rationale must be documented.

---

## Longevity Commitment

HPP is intended to exist for decades.

Design decisions should assume:

- Future hardware evolution
- Adversarial improvement
- Regulatory scrutiny
- Massive scale

Short-term convenience must not override long-term integrity.

---

## Governance Philosophy

HPP is not governed to maximize growth.

HPP is governed to maximize correctness.

Growth is a consequence of correctness.
