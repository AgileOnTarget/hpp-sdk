# Maintainers — Human Presence Protocol (HPP)

This document describes the role, responsibilities, and expectations of maintainers for the Human Presence Protocol (HPP).

Maintainers are stewards of a long-lived protocol, not owners of a product.

---

## Role of Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing and merging specification changes
- Evaluating security implications
- Preserving architectural coherence
- Upholding HPP guiding principles
- Facilitating constructive technical discussion

Maintainers do not dictate direction. They guard correctness.

---

## Responsibilities

Each maintainer is expected to:

- Read and understand the full HPP specification
- Review pull requests within a reasonable time
- Provide clear technical feedback
- Disclose potential conflicts of interest
- Recuse themselves when appropriate

---

## Required Qualities

Maintainers should demonstrate:

- Strong protocol design intuition
- Cryptographic literacy
- Secure systems engineering experience
- Respect for privacy-preserving design
- Ability to reason adversarially

---

## Selection Process

Maintainers are invited by existing maintainers.

Selection is based on:

- Sustained high-quality contributions
- Thoughtful review participation
- Alignment with HPP principles

There is no fixed term. Maintainers may step down voluntarily.

---

## Removal

Maintainers may be removed by consensus of the Maintainer Council for:

- Repeated failure to perform duties
- Security negligence
- Abusive behavior
- Conflicts of interest that cannot be mitigated

---

## Decision Authority

Maintainers:

- Approve or reject pull requests
- Participate in consensus discussions
- Escalate major changes to the Maintainer Council

No single maintainer has unilateral authority over major changes.

---

## Areas of Stewardship

Maintainers may focus on one or more areas:

- Specification text
- Cryptography
- Secure hardware integration
- Verifier architecture
- Privacy design
- Tooling

Specialization is encouraged.

---

## Communication

Primary communication occurs via:

- GitHub Issues
- GitHub Pull Requests

Private channels are reserved for security disclosures.

---

## Code of Conduct

Professional, respectful, technically focused.

No marketing. No hype. No harassment.
